# ♟️ Aetheria: Sovereign Shatter Chess

> A stunning, fully playable 3D FIDE chess engine and interactive WebGL application featuring procedural stone assets, destructive shatter physics, and switchable pastel realms. 
> 
> **Created & Engineered by SOU.**

---

## 🌟 About Aetheria

**Aetheria: Sovereign Shatter Chess** elevates the traditional game of chess into a spatial, immersive experience. Built entirely from scratch using **Three.js** and **Tailwind CSS**, it combines a complete legal chess ruleset and minimax AI automaton with high-end 3D graphics, procedural stone textures, custom Web Audio sound effects, and real-time capture destruction physics.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Complete Legal Chess Engine:** Includes full FIDE rules, castling, en passant, pawn promotion, check, checkmate, and stalemate detection.
* **Minimax AI Automaton:** Test your skills against an intelligent AI opponent with multiple difficulty tiers (Novice, Adept, and Archmage).
* **Destructive Shatter Physics:** Capturing pieces triggers procedural 3D stone explosion physics, complete with flying shards, shockwave rings, and screen shake effects.
* **7 Switchable Pastel Realms:** Instantly switch between atmospheric 3D environments including *Pastel Sanctuary*, *Dream Night Glade*, *Morning Sky Citadel*, *Mint Blossom Meadow*, *Aqua Pastel Lagoon*, *Lilac Astral Nebula*, and *Peach Blush Dunes*.
* **Cross-Platform Controls:** Optimized for both **PC/Laptop** (Mouse click, WASD/Arrow keys, and Q/E camera orbit) and **Mobile/Touch Devices** (Touch tap selection, pinch-to-zoom, and floating D-Pad controllers).
* **Immersive Web Audio Synthesizer:** Programmatically generated sound effects for piece selection, stone slides, charge-ups, and check alerts without external audio dependencies.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Graphics:** Three.js (WebGL renderer with soft shadow maps and ACES Filmic tone mapping).
* **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts (*Cinzel*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *JetBrains Mono*).
* **Audio:** Native Web Audio API (Synthesizers and custom oscillators).
* **Architecture:** Vanilla JavaScript (Zero external build steps or dependencies).

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open `Aetheria.html` in any modern web browser.
2. Select your control platform (**Smartphone/Touch** or **PC/Laptop**).
3. Choose your preferred **Pastel Sanctuary Environment** and click **Enter Aetheria Sanctuary**.
4. Click or navigate using your keyboard/D-Pad to select pieces and execute legal moves!
